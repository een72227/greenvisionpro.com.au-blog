## Summary

The Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program is a Victorian Government scheme designed to help
households and businesses reduce energy consumption by upgrading to more efficient equipment.
**Eligible Victorian households may qualify** for a discount on selected upgrades — such as
reverse cycle heating and cooling, hot water heat pumps, water-saving showerheads and weather
sealing — through accredited providers. Eligibility and discount amounts vary and are not
guaranteed for every household.

## How the VEU program works

The VEU program operates through a certificate trading scheme. When an accredited provider
installs an eligible energy-efficient product, they can create Victorian Energy Efficiency
Certificates (VEECs) based on the estimated energy savings of that upgrade. These certificates
are sold on an open market, and the value generated is typically used to reduce the cost passed
on to the household.

This is why upgrade costs can vary significantly between providers and over time — certificate
prices move with market conditions, and not every product or installation generates the same
certificate value.

## Who administers the program

The VEU program is regulated by the Essential Services Commission (ESC), the independent
Victorian body responsible for overseeing energy and water regulation. Providers must be
accredited by the ESC to participate, and the program sets strict rules about which products
qualify, how they must be installed, and how savings are calculated.

## What upgrades are typically included

Products commonly available through the VEU program include:

- **Reverse cycle air conditioners** for space heating and cooling
- **Heat pump hot water systems** replacing electric resistance or gas storage systems
- **Water-efficient showerheads** that reduce hot water use
- **Draught sealing and weather sealing** products that reduce air leakage
- **Efficient lighting**, where applicable under current program rules

Product lists and eligibility criteria are reviewed periodically by the Victorian Government, so
what is available can change from year to year.

## Am I eligible?

Eligibility is assessed on a case-by-case basis and typically depends on:

- Your **postcode** and whether your address falls within the program's service area
- Your **property type** (owner-occupied, rental, and some commercial premises may be treated
  differently)
- The **equipment currently installed** in your home, since some upgrades require replacing an
  existing, less efficient system
- Whether the **specific product** you're considering is currently listed as eligible

Because these factors interact, the only reliable way to know whether your household qualifies
is to speak with an accredited provider who can assess your specific circumstances.

## What to check before agreeing to an upgrade

- Confirm the provider's **ESC accreditation**
- Ask for a **clear, itemised quote** showing the VEEC contribution separately from installation
  costs
- Understand the **product warranty** and who is responsible for servicing
- Ask what happens if your **eligibility changes** partway through the process

## Why this matters for Victorian households

Energy-efficient upgrades reduce the amount of energy a household needs to heat, cool and heat
water — which, for many households, translates into lower ongoing running costs and reduced
environmental impact. The VEU program exists to make these upgrades more accessible, but it is
not a blanket entitlement, and outcomes depend on individual circumstances.
